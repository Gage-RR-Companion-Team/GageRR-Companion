# Gage R&R Companion
A companion tool to assist in developing Gage R&R studies.

### What is Gage R&R?
Gage Repeatability and Reproducibility (Gage R&R) is a process used to evaluage a gauging insturments accuracy by ensuring its measurements are repeatable and reproducible

### What does Gage R&R companion do?
This program is meant to act as a tool to assist in developing Gage R&R studies in an efficient manner by taking in various variables and providing the most efficient route to completing the study. It also assists in understanding the results of a Gage R&R study through various equations.
